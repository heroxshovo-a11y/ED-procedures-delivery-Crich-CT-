# ED Pocket v2.0 — 24-topic safety triage and specialist-review queue

**Scope:** All 24 reference chapters were indexed and reviewed for high-risk themes, provenance gaps, and alignment with selected verified primary guidelines. This is an AI-assisted source/risk triage, **not** 24 specialist-approved clinical audits. Dosing tables and procedural technique have **not** been independently validated.

**Disposition:** 0/24 clinician-approved. 24/24 have a review owner category and specific verification question. Clinical release blocked.

## High-risk discrepancies and cautions

- **Hepatitis B prophylaxis:** CDC December 2025 and AAP 2026 recommendations differ for infants of HBsAg-negative parents; a visible policy-divergence note was added. Never apply the negative-status pathway to positive or unknown status.
- **Neonatal resuscitation:** effective ventilation and spontaneous breathing remain independent decisions even if HR is ≥100; verify airway skill/NRP algorithm.
- **Postpartum hemorrhage:** WHO 2025 uses ≥500 mL or ≥300 mL with a hemodynamic abnormality; do not make a negative threshold a rule-out.
- **Material limitation:** Medication doses, device sizes, and complex maneuvers are flagged for specialist/pharmacy review rather than silently altered.

## Checklist required for each topic before clinician approval

1. Named licensed specialty reviewer, date, and version recorded.
2. Verify every medication, route, timing, device detail, contraindication, and limitation with source and local formulary.
3. Confirm all emergency escalation, staffing and transport procedures against each institution.
4. Run simulation-based usability test; verify warnings and failure modes.
5. Re-review any subsequent clinically meaningful change before production promotion.
